ABOUT JOBMATCH

Data-driven candidate matching

JobMatch is an educational ML application for comparing job requirements with candidate information.

What the application does

You can store jobs and candidates, upload a CSV dataset, train Logistic Regression and Random Forest models, compare Accuracy, Precision, Recall and F1 Score, and run a saved-model prediction.

Important: the percentage is a model-generated eligibility/match probability. It is not a guarantee of hiring or employment.
